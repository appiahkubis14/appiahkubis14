## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Geospatial Intelligence Engineer** and **AI Researcher** based in Ghana, working at the intersection of Earth observation, deep learning, and software engineering. My research focuses on making precision geospatial intelligence accessible across Africa — from smartphone-based road assessment to satellite monitoring of tropical forests.

**Research interests:** Multi-sensor fusion · SAR-optical integration · Edge AI for EO · Foundation models for Earth observation · Digital twins for environmental monitoring

**Currently:** Writing papers for IEEE T-ITS, Remote Sensing, and Computers & Electronics in Agriculture.

**Open to:** PhD positions (EO/AI), research collaborations, and software engineering roles in geospatial tech.

## 🔬 Selected Research Projects

| Project | Description | Key Contribution | Status |
|---------|-------------|------------------|--------|
| **MMFF** | Multi-modal fusion framework for pothole dimensioning using smartphone camera + IMU. No LiDAR. | Depth MAE 0.28 m · 30 fps · 79% jitter reduction | *IEEE T-ITS* (in prep) |
| **Afarinsene** | Production CNN for cocoa disease (CSSVD) detection deployed to 1,000+ farmers across Ghana & Côte d'Ivoire | 95.8% accuracy · < 1 min diagnosis | *Comput. Electron. Agric.* (in prep) |
| **Atewa Mining Detector** | Sentinel-1/2 + UAV fusion for illegal mining detection in 23,663 ha tropical forest reserve | 82% recall · SAR cloud-penetrating | *Remote Sensing* (in prep) |
| **SMART‑FOREST** | Self-modelling digital twin for forest carbon dynamics using OBIA + T‑GNN | 10‑year AGB forecasting · REDD+ compliant | Master's thesis |
| **Coastwatch‑Erosion** | Swin‑UNETR + Bayesian LSTM for shoreline erosion forecasting in West Africa | 6‑month forecast · COG + STAC export | Open source |

*Full list: 10+ production‑grade repositories on [GitHub](https://github.com/appiahkubis14)*
