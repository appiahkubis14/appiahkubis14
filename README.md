### PyGeoFetch — Universal Satellite Data Pipeline

[![PyPI](https://img.shields.io/pypi/v/pygeofetch?color=ea580c&label=PyPI&logo=pypi&logoColor=white)](https://pypi.org/project/pygeofetch/)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-1a1a1a?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/appiahkubis14/pygeofetch/blob/main/LICENSE)

Search and download satellite imagery from **22+ global providers** with a single command. Supports USGS, Copernicus, Planet, Maxar, NASA Earthdata, Planetary Computer, Sentinel Hub, Airbus, OpenTopography, and more. Built with keyring authentication, parallel downloads, checksum verification, resume support, YAML pipeline orchestration, webhook notifications, and 10+ post-processing actions including NDVI, COG, and pan-sharpen.

📖 [Documentation](https://github.com/appiahkubis14/pygeofetch) · 📦 [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pygeofetch/)

### PyGeoVision — World-Class Geospatial AI Platform

[![PyPI](https://img.shields.io/pypi/v/pygeovision?color=ea580c&label=PyPI&logo=pypi&logoColor=white)](https://pypi.org/project/pygeovision/)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache_2.0-1a1a1a?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/appiahkubis14/PyGeoVision/blob/main/LICENSE)
[![Tests](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tests-208_passing-ea580c?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/appiahkubis14/PyGeoVision)

A production platform unifying satellite data acquisition and geospatial AI in a single coherent API. Integrates **22+ satellite providers**, **24 GeoAI subsystems** (segmentation, detection, change detection, SAM, NASA Prithvi, DINOv3), **14 model architectures**, **7 automated labelers**, and **10 end-to-end pipelines** covering building footprints, land cover, deforestation, disaster assessment, crop monitoring, carbon estimation, and more.

📖 [Documentation](https://github.com/appiahkubis14/PyGeoVision) · 📦 [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pygeovision/)

### raster2pm — One-Command GeoTIFF to PMTiles Converter

[![PyPI](https://img.shields.io/pypi/v/raster2pm?color=ea580c&label=PyPI&logo=pypi&logoColor=white)](https://pypi.org/project/raster2pm/)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-1a1a1a?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/appiahkubis14/raster2pm/blob/main/LICENSE)

Convert any GeoTIFF — orthomosaics, NDVI, NDRE, satellite imagery — to web-optimised PMTiles in a single command. Features smart colormap auto-detection for NDVI and NDRE indices, memory-efficient block-by-block processing tested on **64 GB orthomosaics**, parallel multi-core conversion, custom zoom levels, and output compatible with MapLibre, Leaflet, OpenLayers, and pmtiles.io.

📖 [Documentation](https://github.com/appiahkubis14/raster2pm) · 📦 [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/raster2pm/)

## Research

**Multi-Modal Fusion Framework (MMFF)** — *In preparation · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems*

A five-component sensor fusion architecture fusing monocular camera, IMU, and GPS for metric-accurate road infrastructure assessment at 30 fps without LiDAR. Validated across 50 manually surveyed ground-truth measurements on 50 km of diverse road surfaces. Depth MAE 0.28 m · 62% error reduction · 48.9% area-error reduction · 79% jitter reduction.
